How it works

🍲
1. Donor posts food

Restaurants, hostels and event organisers add quantity, pickup time and storage details.

🤝
2. A community partner claims it

An NGO or volunteer coordinates collection before the deadline.

✓
3. Handover is recorded

Both sides confirm collection and the rescued quantity is counted.

Food safety first

Only share food that has been handled and stored safely. Add clear allergen and preparation details, and never donate food that may be unsafe.
